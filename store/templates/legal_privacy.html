Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 28, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly: name, email address, shipping address, and payment details when you place an order. We also automatically collect browsing data (IP address, browser type, pages visited) through cookies and similar technologies.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information to process and fulfil orders, send order confirmations and shipping updates, respond to customer service requests, improve our website, and send promotional communications (with your consent).

3. Sharing Your Information

We share your information with third-party suppliers and fulfilment partners (including CJ Dropshipping and AliExpress partners) solely to process and ship your order. We also share data with payment processors (Stripe) and shipping carriers. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Data Retention

We retain your order and account information for 7 years for tax and legal compliance. You may request deletion of your data by contacting us; we will honour requests unless retention is required by law.

5. Cookies

We use essential cookies for cart functionality and analytics cookies (with your consent) to understand site usage. You can disable non-essential cookies in your browser settings.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or port your personal data. Contact us at the email below to exercise these rights.

7. Contact

Email: support@mydropshop.com. We will respond within 5 business days.
