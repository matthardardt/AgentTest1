The core challenge

Gross margin is healthy — acquisition is where plans die

A naive paid-only launch loses money on every sale: at a $0.90 cost-per-click and a 1.0% conversion rate, each order costs $70 to acquire, but only contributes a fraction of that. That baseline plan never breaks even and burns $-54,445 in year one.

The unit-economics identity the analyst enforces:

Paid CAC = Cost-per-click ÷ Conversion rate  →  profit needs Contribution per order > CAC, or repeat purchases must carry it.

Levers: raise AOV, lift conversion, cut effective CPC with better creative, shift mix toward organic/email, and grow repeat purchase (zero-CAC revenue).

The strategy

Five levers — one per agent

The Business Analyst issues specific, quantified recommendations; each operational agent acts on the ones addressed to it. This is how the plan moves from "loses money" to "signed off."

Pricing
Introduce a $50 free-shipping threshold and bundle pricing to lift AOV from $35 → $44, while holding the 1.25× margin floor.
Product Hunting
Favour higher-AOV, bundleable products in the $25–$60 band; retire zero-order SKUs.
Website
Add trust signals, reviews, and clearer benefit copy to push conversion 1.3% → 3.1%.
Design
Higher-converting ad creative and trust badges to drive effective CPC down to $0.75.
Manager
Stand up email/retention and organic channels — lifting repeat purchase and cutting blended CAC to $17.

Diligence

Key assumptions & risks

Assumptions
  • Conversion 1.3% → 3.1% as trust/UX improve
  • AOV $44 blended via bundles + free-ship threshold
  • ~70% product gross margin held by the pricing agent
  • Organic/email grows to ~21,993 sessions/mo, cutting blended CAC to $17
  • 18% of customers reorder monthly at maturity
Risks & mitigations
  • Rising CPC / ad fatigue → design agent refreshes creative; lean on organic.
  • Conversion underperforms → website agent A/Bs copy; pricing tightens offers.
  • Supplier delays/defects → ordering agent tracks SLAs; 3% returns reserve.
  • Thin early cash → fund the J-curve trough (~$4,746).
  • Platform/compliance → legal pages live; Stripe + webhook verified.
